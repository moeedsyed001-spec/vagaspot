# 19 — Privacy Notice and Location Consent Copy (Draft)

This is product copy for the private pilot, not legal advice or a substitute for reviewing applicable law before public release.

## 1. Plain-language privacy notice

VAGASPOT helps your invited friend group share campus presence, availability and a selected campus zone.

### Information the app uses
- Your Google account identifier, display name and optional profile image URL.
- Squad membership and invitation state.
- Your latest selected campus presence, availability and campus zone.
- Timestamps needed to show how recently your presence was updated and when manual statuses expire.
- Notification preferences and, if Android push is enabled, your device's FCM registration token.
- In Release 2 only, Android may use background location permissions to detect entry into or exit from the configured COMSATS campus boundary.

### Information the app does not intend to store
- A continuous GPS trail or movement history.
- A history of your exact coordinates.
- Your home address, phone number, student ID or date of birth.
- GPS coordinates in friend cards or notifications.

### Who can see your shared presence
Only active members of the same private squad can see your visible presence. Ghost Mode keeps your name/avatar in the member list but hides campus state, availability, zone, source and timestamps. Squad owners do not get an exception.

### Your controls
You can use manual check-in without granting background location. On Android, automatic campus detection is optional and can be disabled. You can change notification preferences, use Ghost Mode, leave a squad or request account deletion.

### Important limitation
Information a friend saw before you enabled Ghost Mode may have been remembered, copied or screenshotted. Ghost Mode prevents new access through the app; it cannot erase another person's prior knowledge.

## 2. Android background location disclosure (show before asking OS permission)

**Suggested copy:**

“VAGASPOT can optionally detect when you enter or leave the COMSATS Islamabad campus so your squad can see whether you're on campus even when you forget to check in. This uses Android's geofencing/location services in the background. We send your campus state to VAGASPOT, not a continuous location trail. Your exact coordinates are not saved as a movement history. You can turn this off at any time, use manual check-in instead, or enable Ghost Mode to hide your presence. Automatic detection can be delayed or missed by Android, battery settings, permission or network problems.”

Buttons:
- “Enable automatic campus detection”
- “Not now — use manual check-in”

Do not preselect background permission without user action. Explain that Android may require the user to visit Settings to grant “Allow all the time”. If permission is denied, the rest of the app remains usable.

## 3. Notification permission explanation

“Allow VAGASPOT notifications to hear when friends arrive on campus or become free. You can turn off any notification category or all notifications at any time. Notifications use generic text and will not include a hidden location.”

## 4. Pilot consent checklist

Before enabling Release 2, ask every friend to confirm they understand:
- automation is optional;
- campus events can be delayed or missed;
- the app does not prove attendance;
- exact GPS movement history is not part of the product;
- Ghost Mode hides future access, not information already seen;
- permission can be revoked in Android Settings.
